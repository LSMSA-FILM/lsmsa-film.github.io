Using OBS Studio

tip

This page is very long! Navigate this page using the Table of Contents to the right.

Things you need​

Required​

  • A personal laptop with OBS Studio installed and set up (see OBS Studio)
  • A tripod (to hold the camera)
  • A camera that can connect to the laptop. Options, ordered from best to worst, include:
    • A webcam that can be screwed onto a tripod (easiest)
      • You can borrow a Logitech webcam that works well from the IT department
    • A camera that can output through HDMI or NDI (hardest, but best looking)
      • This requires you to know how to input these into your device. You will need either an HDMI capture card or a networking port on your device.
    • Your laptop's webcam (worst)
      • Please don't consider this option

Optional​

  • A table to put the laptop on
  • A chair to sit on
  • A microphone that can connect to your laptop
    • This can be used for commentary
  • An additional camera
    • This can be used to point towards your commentators
  • An extension cord and your laptop charger
    • Livestreaming takes up a lot of battery, and there is no way around this
    • The gym has a few good extension cords laying around, ask the coaches.

Steps: Before the event​

Below are things you should complete before you actually start livestreaming.

Setting up the livestream event​

important

If you don't set up your livestream event, your livestream's title and thumbnail may appear incorrectly!

To livestream on YouTube, you can do one of the following:

  1. Schedule a stream event
  2. Manually set up your livestream every time

Select the option you wish to do in the tabs below:

  • Scheduled
  • Manual
tip

If you wish to schedule your livestream, you should complete the following steps at some point before you start livestreaming. We recommend doing this at least a day in advance.

  1. Open the YouTube livestream management page. To do this, head to youtube.com, sign in, click the "Create" button at the top right, and select "Go live"

  2. If this is your first time, YouTube may show an onboarding modal. You can follow the steps if you'd like. If it prompts you if the video is made for kids, make sure to select "No, it's not made for kids"

  3. On the left sidebar, head to the Manage tab (should be the third option)

  4. Click "Schedule Stream" on the top right

Location of Schedule Stream button

  1. Enter in the title of the livestream and, optionally, a description.

  2. Ensure the option entitled "How do you want to go live?" is set to "Streaming software"

  3. Ensure the Category is set to Sports

  4. For the thumbnail, upload a thumbnail from here: Athletics Stream/Video Thumbnails

  5. For the option that asks whether your stream is made for kids, select the option "No, it's not made for kids"

  6. Click Next on the bottom right. You will see some customization options, which you can change if you want (the defaults work fine).

  7. Click Next.

  8. Set the livestream as Public (so that anybody can watch it)

  9. For the Schedule, select the date and time that the livestream will start. Make sure this is accurate!

  10. When you click Done, the livestream will be scheduled and appear on your YouTube channel as an upcoming livestream.

  1. Open the YouTube livestream management page. To do this, head to youtube.com, sign in, click the "Create" button at the top right, and select "Go live"

  2. If this is your first time, YouTube may show an onboarding modal. You can follow the steps if you'd like. If it prompts you if the video is made for kids, make sure to select "No, it's not made for kids"

  3. On the left sidebar, head to the Stream tab (should be the first option)

  4. Click the Edit button that appears to the right of the stream title at the top

  1. Enter in the title of the livestream and, optionally, a description.

  2. Ensure the option entitled "How do you want to go live?" is set to "Streaming software"

  3. Set the livestream as Public (so that anybody can watch it)

  4. Ensure the Category is set to Sports

  5. For the thumbnail, upload a thumbnail from here: Athletics Stream/Video Thumbnails

  6. For the option that asks whether your stream is made for kids, select the option "No, it's not made for kids"

  7. If you wish to customize the livestream, go to the "Customization" tab on the left side of the modal (the defaults work fine, though).

  8. On the bottom right, click Save

General setup​

tip

Make sure to complete the following at least 30 minutes before the event starts!

  1. Set up a table and chairs in the gym. Where you place the table will depend on what sport is being played. Here are some recommended locations:

    • For volleyball: Place the table by the door that faces the CPT (you will need an extension cord that goes behind the bleachers if you want to charge your devices)
    • For basketball: Place the table on the opposite corner, by the equipment room

  2. If you wish to charge your devices (which you should, since livestreaming takes a lot of battery), plug an extension cord to an outlet and lead it to the table.

  3. Set up your tripod and attach the camera to the top of it. Make sure it is next to the table so that the cable for the camera can plug into your laptop.

  4. Set up your laptop on the table, plug in the charger, and open OBS Studio. Plug in your camera and ensure it shows up correctly.

  5. If you are using a scoreboard software, get that ready as well.

  6. Open the YouTube livestream management page. To do this, head to youtube.com, sign in, click the "Create" button at the top right, and select "Go live"

  7. If you haven't set up a scheduled stream, you will need to set up the livestream before you start. On the left, sidebar, head to the "Stream" tab (should be the first option). Click the Edit button that appears to the right of the stream title at the top, then fill in the information on the modal that appears (make sure to scroll down so you can upload a thumbnail!)

  8. If you set up OBS Studio according to OBS Setup, continue with the Recommended tab below. Otherwise, continue with the Manual tab.

  • Recommended
  • Manual

  1. Navigate back to OBS Studio.

  2. To the right of the "Start streaming" button, there should be a button called "Manage Broadcast". Click this.

  3. At the top, go to the Select Existing Broadcast tab. Here, you should see a list of your scheduled streams that you made on YouTube. Select the event you wish to stream, then hit "Select broadcast" at the bottom.

warning

Careful! If you hit "Select broadcast and start streaming", your livestream will start Immediately!

  1. You should have a panel called YouTube Live Control Panel.
  • If you don't see this panel, go to the menu bar -> Docks -> YouTube Live Control Panel

  1. Grab the correct stream key (so you are streaming to the correct livestream). There are two cases:

    • If you are streaming WITHOUT a scheduled stream, copy the "Stream key" on the Stream tab (near the bottom left) to your clipboard.
    • If you are streaming with a scheduled stream, open the correct scheduled livestream on the Manage tab. Then, copy the "Stream key" to your clipboard

  2. Navigate back to OBS Studio

  3. Open OBS settings (button called Settings at the bottom right). When the settings modal appears, navigate to the "Stream" tab.

  4. Under Service, select "Custom..."

  5. Paste in your stream key under "Stream key"

  6. For the destination server, go back to the YouTube page, copy the Stream URL underneath the stream key, go back to OBS, and paste the server URL.

  7. IMPORTANT! At the bottom right, click Apply, then close the settingsaA

Optional commentary setup​

If you're doing commentary, you may do the following:

  1. Plug a microphone into your laptop

  2. Plug an additional camera into your laptop

Optional scoreboard setup​

If you're displaying a scoreboard on the stream, make sure to set it up.

Steps: During the event​

Once you've completed everything in the previous sections, you should be all set up! You can hit "Start Streaming" to begin the livestream.
