# Ready to start livestreaming?

Welcome! If you're hoping to livestream an event for LSMSA and are unsure how to do that, you've found yourself in the right place! This documentation consists of many pages that cover a wide variety of topics that hope to teach you how to livestream.

:::info

This documentation was written with the [Louisiana School for the Math, Science, and the Arts](https://www.lsmsa.edu/) (LSMSA) in mind, but many of the workflows described here are probably applicable outside of LSMSA as well.

:::

## Why is there so much information?

You probably don't need to read everything here. Thus, don't be overwhelmed by how much information is in this documentation! If you're confused on how to get started, **start at [Quick Start](livestreaming/getting-started/quick-start.md) and continue from there.**

## Who wrote this documentation?

Nearly everything here was written by Isaac Young, an alum of LSMSA from the Class of 2025. He wrote much of this as a part of his work service at the IT department. Isaac managed most of the student-run livestreaming during the 2024–25 school year, and he hopes to share everything he learned with you so that LSMSA continues to be welcoming to family and friends who want to attend LSMSA events, yet are unable to travel to watch them in-person.

:::info

As of the time of writing (2025), LSMSA has not yet finished construction of their dedicated broadcasting room located in the Gym/SAC. Because Isaac graduated after the broadcasting room was finished, this documentation will not cover information on how to operate the broadcasting room.

If you are a future student reading this, please help update this documentation! You may contribute on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/LSMSA-FILM/lsmsa-film.github.io).

If you do have questions about the broadcasting room, please contact the [LSMSA IT department](mailto:it@lsmsa.edu).

:::
